About the Game

Gunita: Reaper of Echoes is an AI-powered narrative adventure that brings Philippine history and culture to life through exploration, investigation, and storytelling.

As Vino, the newly appointed Echo Reaper, you journey into fragmented Memory Crystals to uncover forgotten stories, restore lost artifacts, and guide wandering souls toward peace.

Every restored memory helps preserve Philippine heritage for future generations.

The dead do not seek to be feared… only remembered.

Story

Long ago, every story that shaped the nation was preserved within the TALA, the Hall of Remembrance.

As generations passed, traditions faded, legends disappeared, and countless memories were forgotten.

Now those forgotten stories survive only as restless souls wandering Campo Lunan.

You are Vino, an Echo Reaper entrusted with restoring these lost memories before they vanish forever.

What is forgotten may still echo — if only someone will listen.

The World

Campo Lunan

A quiet, moonlit cemetery at the edge of memory. Rows of glowing graves mark the souls waiting to be remembered. Each grave is a doorway into a life once lived.

Here you will meet wandering spirits, discover fragment clues, and choose whose story you will restore next.

Memory Worlds

Within each grave lies a Memory World — a reconstructed vision of the past shaped from the fragments of a soul's recollection.

Villages, forests, shorelines, and homes from bygone eras wait to be explored once more.

Every grave is a book; every world is a chapter.

Characters

Vino — The Echo Reaper

A young man chosen as the bridge between the living and the lost. Gifted with the Echo ability, Vino can hear what others cannot and shine light on memories buried by time.

The Wandering Souls

Fishermen, wives, children, villagers, dreamers — ordinary people whose extraordinary stories faded with the years.

Each soul carries fragments of history, love, regret, and hope that only you can piece back together.

Aling Sisa — The Shopkeeper

A familiar presence between worlds, running Aling Sisa's Tindahan. She trades Sentimos for healing, hints, and small comforts for your journey.

Behind every name on a stone is a life worth remembering.

Your Mission

For every soul:

  • Enter its Memory World
  • Explore the environment
  • Collect every Memory Fragment
  • Solve AI-generated riddles
  • Restore forgotten artifacts
  • Discover the truth behind the soul's story
  • Help the soul find peace
You're not here to defeat monsters. You're here to remember.

How to Play

Step 1

Explore Campo Lunan. Choose a glowing grave to investigate.

Step 2

Enter the Memory World. Search every area carefully. Hidden paths may contain important memories.

Step 3

Use the Echo Ability (Space). Echo reveals nearby Memory Fragments. Watch for glowing signals.

Step 4

Interact with a Memory Fragment and answer the AI-generated riddle correctly.

Correct Answer: Restore the artifact and learn part of the soul's story.

Wrong Answer: Lose Essence, the Fragment relocates, and you must search again.

Step 5

Collect every Memory Fragment. Only then can the soul's true story be revealed.

Game Mechanics

Echo

Your supernatural ability. Detects nearby hidden memories and clues. Use it often.

Memory Fragments

Scattered pieces of forgotten history. Each restored fragment reveals artifacts, dialogue, story clues, and historical context.

Essence

Your spiritual connection to the Memory World.

Lose Essence by incorrect answers, environmental hazards, and investigation mistakes.

If Essence reaches 0, the Memory World collapses and the investigation restarts.

Sentimos

Crystallized emotions scattered throughout Memory Worlds.

Collect them to purchase healing items, hint items, and cosmetics from Aling Sisa's Tindahan.

Tips for New Reapers

  • Explore every corner.
  • Use Echo frequently.
  • Read dialogue carefully.
  • Observe your surroundings.
  • Every clue matters.
  • Do not rush your answers.
  • Every artifact tells part of the story.
Patience is the light by which the past is best seen.
